Address the need for counseling

Providers are the front line for youth nicotine treatment. Deliver one-on-one counseling in your own practice, or refer to one-on-one counseling — then add medication when it is indicated.

NDQuits (1-800-QUIT-NOW) is a feasible option for patients ages 18–21. It is not intended as the treatment resource for younger youth.
